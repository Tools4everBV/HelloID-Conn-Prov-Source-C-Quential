## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2025-10-02

This is an initial pre-release of **HelloID-Conn-Prov-Target-C-Quential**.

**Notes:**
- Connector is partially developed; not yet production-ready.
- `person.ps1` is completed, but date range filtering requires validation.
- `departments.ps1` and `mapping.json` are not yet developed.
- Helper functions (`Invoke-C-QuentialRestMethod` and `Resolve-CQuentialError`) are present but unfinished; paging and error handling enhancements are needed.
- Intended for testing, feedback, and early validation.
